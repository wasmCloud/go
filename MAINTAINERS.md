# MAINTAINERS

The following people maintain this repository.
They review and merge pull requests, triage issues,
and cut releases.
This document is organized by the GitHub team
that can be used to mention _all_ maintainers of that area.

The roles and responsibilities of maintainers,
and how someone becomes one,
are in the wasmCloud [governance](https://github.com/wasmCloud/wasmCloud/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
Maintainers of the rest of the wasmCloud project are in the wasmCloud
[MAINTAINERS.md](https://github.com/wasmCloud/wasmCloud/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md).
